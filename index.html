Who We Are

Lughat is an offline-first Urdu ⇄ English dictionary application developed and maintained by Ateeq. The app is designed to help readers, learners, and writers look up words in both Urdu and English — even without an internet connection.

This Privacy Policy explains how Lughat handles information in connection with your use of the app. By using Lughat, you agree to the practices described below.

2

Information We Do Not Collect

✅

Lughat does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to us or any third party. There are no analytics libraries, advertising SDKs, or crash-reporting services embedded in the app.

Specifically, we do not collect:

Category Examples Collected?
Personal identifiers Name, email address, phone number Never
Device identifiers IMEI, advertising ID, device fingerprint Never
Location data GPS, IP-based location, region Never
Usage analytics Screen views, button taps, session duration Never
Crash reports Stack traces, error logs sent to a server Never
Financial information Payment details, purchase history Never
3

Data Stored on Your Device

Lughat stores a small amount of data locally on your device using Android's / iOS's sandboxed SharedPreferences storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

What is stored Purpose Leaves device?
Favorite words So you can access saved words offline No
Recent searches Quick access to words you looked up previously No
Word of the Day history Deterministic daily word based on calendar date No
Learned & review words Spaced-repetition review schedule No
API word cache Offline access to previously looked-up definitions No
App settings Theme preference (light/dark/system) No
Onboarding flag Tracks whether first-run onboarding has been shown No

You can clear this data at any time from Settings → Clear word cache or by uninstalling the app.

4

Network Requests

Lughat is designed to work offline. When you are connected to the internet and explicitly search for a word, the app makes a request to the Free Dictionary API (freedictionaryapi.com) to retrieve its definition.

ℹ️

The search query (the word itself) is included in this API request as part of the URL. The Free Dictionary API is a public, open service. Please review their privacy policy if you have concerns about their data handling.

Lughat itself does not receive or record these requests. The definition response is cached locally on your device for future offline use. No other outbound network connections are made by the app.

6

Children's Privacy

Lughat does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). Because no personal data is collected at all, Lughat is safe for use by children under parental or guardian supervision.

7

Your Rights & Controls

Since Lughat does not collect any personal data, there is nothing held by us to access, correct, export, or delete. All data that exists is stored locally on your device and is entirely within your control.

You can manage your data directly in the app:

  • Clear recent searches — Search screen → "Clear" button
  • Remove favorites — Saved tab → swipe to delete
  • Clear word cache — Settings → Offline Data → "Clear word cache"
  • Delete all data — Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data
